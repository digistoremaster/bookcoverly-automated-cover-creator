## 🎯 The Hidden Challenge of Print Book Covers

Most general-purpose graphic design tools (and even online drag-and-drop editors) are built for digital graphics—not physical printing standards. They don't know:

* **How page count alters spine width**: A 200-page book on cream paper requires a completely different spine width than a 200-page book on white paper.
* **The strict "Bleed & Safe Area" rules**: If your background elements don't extend past the trim line by exact millimeter precision, printing presses will leave ugly white edges.
* **Vector Barcode Requirements**: Lower-resolution PNG barcodes often fail automated distribution center scanners.

In the past, you had two choices: spend hundreds of dollars hiring a cover designer, or waste dozens of hours wrestling with complex canvas settings in Photoshop.
